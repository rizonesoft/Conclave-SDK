# AIOSEO features, tiers and release history as the blog describes them

Scope: this file merges every AIOSEO feature, plan, price and release claim found in the 571 aioseo.com blog posts (2015 to 2026-10-09), as recorded by nine reader agents. It is vendor data, not documentation. Tier and price claims drift between posts, so conflicts are shown side by side with dates and the most recent claim is marked. Menu paths are given as the posts give them; the docs-based references (settings-search-appearance.md, licensing-account.md, ai-mcp.md and the others in this folder) are the authority for click paths and settings. "First / most recent" dates are the earliest and latest post dates in the notes that mention the feature, so a feature can be older than its first date here. Verify any tier at https://aioseo.com/pricing/ before telling a user what a plan includes.
